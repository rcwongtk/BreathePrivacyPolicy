# Delete Account & Data Request

Last updated: April 2026

## How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, please email:

📧 dev@breathepk.com

Include:
- The email associated with your account
- A request stating you want your account deleted

---

## What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon request, we will delete:
- Your Firebase Authentication account (email login)
- Any associated user data stored in Firebase (e.g., database records)

---

## Data Retention

- Deletion requests are processed within 7–14 days
- Some minimal data may be retained temporarily for:
  - Legal compliance
  - Fraud prevention
  - Security purposes

---

## Important Notes

- Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
- You will lose access to all associated app data
